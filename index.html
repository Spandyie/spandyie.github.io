Selected Work

Ama Dablam: A Language Model for Nepali, Maithili, and Bhojpuri

A 322M-parameter language model trained on 85% Devanagari, 5% IAST, and 10% phonetic romanization. Achieves 0.4061 bits-per-byte on held-out Nepali text.

agent-as-judge

Multi-agent evaluation pipeline with three specialized AI agents (Researcher, Analyzer, Judge) that score documents against benchmarks. Runs fully local via Ollama.
